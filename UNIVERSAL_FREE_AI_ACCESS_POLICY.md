# Universal Free AI Access Policy

## Mission
Make AI broadly accessible without requiring users to pay for core capabilities where the project controls the service.

## Principles
- No subscription requirement for core project features.
- Equal access regardless of a user's ability to pay, subject to applicable law and legitimate safety requirements.
- Transparent disclosure of models, limits, third-party dependencies, and data handling.
- Fair-use controls may be used to protect infrastructure and prevent abuse, but must not be used as a hidden paywall.
- The project must not bypass another provider's authentication, pricing, licensing, rate limits, or security controls.
- Prefer efficient, open-source, locally hosted, donated, or otherwise legitimately funded infrastructure to keep access free.
- Free access does not remove safety, privacy, or abuse-prevention requirements.

## Third-Party AI
Independent AI providers control their own pricing and access policies. This project cannot make those services free without the provider's authorization. Integrations must use authorized APIs and comply with their terms.

## Scalability
For large-scale distribution, maintain one canonical source repository and use legitimate build/release pipelines and replicated artifacts rather than creating unnecessary duplicate repositories.

## Governance
Changes affecting access, pricing, privacy, or safety should be documented and reviewed. Users should receive clear notice of material changes.

## Statement
> AI should be broadly available to humanity, while respecting safety, law, provider rights, and sustainable infrastructure.
