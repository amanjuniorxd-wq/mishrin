DONALD J. TRUMP — PUBLIC-RECORD DOSSIER
Research cutoff: 17 September 2026

IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE
This is a public-record research dossier, not literally “all data.” No public source contains every fact about a person from birth to the present. I also cannot obtain, disclose, or compile private messages, private account contents, hacked material, or non-public personal communications. I have excluded private/sensitive information that is not legitimately public. The Epstein section covers publicly released government/court material and reported public evidence; a person's appearance in a document, contact book, photograph, or flight record is not by itself proof of criminal conduct.

1. IDENTITY AND EARLY LIFE
Donald John Trump was born June 14, 1946, in Queens, New York City. He is the son of Fred Trump and Mary Anne MacLeod Trump. He attended the New York Military Academy and later the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School. He entered the family real-estate business and expanded into Manhattan real estate, branding, entertainment, golf, and other ventures.

2. BUSINESS CAREER
Trump's business activities have included real estate development, ownership, leasing, licensing, hotels and hospitality, golf resorts and clubs, commercial and residential branding/licensing, television and entertainment, books, consumer merchandise, digital collectibles, cryptocurrency ventures associated with the Trump family, and other investment/licensing entities.

The Trump Organization is privately held, so complete corporate accounts are not publicly available in the way they would be for a public company. Public financial disclosures therefore provide ranges and reported income categories rather than a complete consolidated corporate income statement.

3. PRESIDENCY
Trump served as the 45th President of the United States from January 20, 2017 to January 20, 2021, and returned as the 47th President on January 20, 2025.

The statutory presidential salary is $400,000 per year. Presidential compensation is separate from income Trump may receive from assets or businesses, subject to applicable disclosure and ethics rules.

4. PUBLIC FINANCIAL DISCLOSURES
The U.S. Office of Government Ethics publishes presidential financial disclosure reports. OGE states that Form 278e reports are designed to disclose financial interests required by the Ethics in Government Act; they are not a complete corporate accounting of every family asset.

A certified 2025 annual financial disclosure was released June 30, 2026.

Major 2025 income reported/reported in coverage of the disclosure included:
- More than $1.4 billion in income associated with crypto ventures.
- Nearly $800 million associated with World Liberty Financial, including more than $520 million from token sales and about $250 million from sales of interests in the business.
- About $635 million associated with Trump meme-coin sales.
- More than $500 million from golf resorts and related traditional businesses, with Mar-a-Lago reported at about $77 million.
- More than $80 million from media settlements and about $52 million from international branding deals, according to Reuters' review of the disclosure.

These figures should not be interpreted as Trump's personal cash salary. Financial-disclosure income can represent revenue, distributions, gains, or other reportable categories, and ownership is divided among entities and family members.

5. FAMILY BUSINESS ACTIVITY SINCE 2024
Public reporting since 2024 documents substantial business activity by Trump's adult sons and affiliated family entities.

World Liberty Financial launched before the 2024 election and is associated with Donald Trump and his sons. Public reporting describes substantial economic interests/income through affiliated entities.

$TRUMP meme coin launched in January 2025. Public reporting has described hundreds of millions of dollars in economic proceeds connected to Trump-affiliated entities; gross trading volume or revenue should not be confused with personal profit.

Donald Trump Jr. continued Trump Organization involvement and became a partner at 1789 Capital in 2024. Eric Trump continued Trump Organization involvement and participated in investment/SPAC-related ventures.

6. EPSTEIN: PUBLICLY RELEASED RECORD
Jeffrey Epstein was a convicted sex offender who was later federally charged with sex trafficking. Ghislaine Maxwell was convicted in 2021 of offenses connected to Epstein's sexual-abuse scheme and sentenced to 20 years.

Trump and Epstein were socially acquainted for years. Publicly released records document social photographs and flights involving Trump and Epstein.

A DOJ-released record from January 2020 states that investigators reviewing flight records found Trump listed as a passenger on at least eight Epstein-plane flights between 1993 and 1996, including at least four flights on which Ghislaine Maxwell was also listed. The same record says passengers on different flights included Marla Maples, Tiffany Trump, and Eric Trump. The document is an internal prosecutor communication describing flight records; it is not itself a finding that Trump committed a crime.

The DOJ's February 27, 2025 release included Epstein flight logs, a redacted contact book, and other records.

The DOJ's January 30, 2026 release said it had published more than 3 million additional pages, including more than 2,000 videos and 180,000 images, bringing the total production to nearly 3.5 million pages. DOJ also warned that the production could contain fake or falsely submitted material because public submissions to the FBI were included. Victim-identifying information and certain legally protected material were redacted.

IMPORTANT EPSTEIN LIMITATION:
“Epstein files” are not a single verified list of offenders or participants. The collection contains government records, court records, tips, correspondence, photographs, flight records, and other material. Presence of a name or image does not establish wrongdoing. Claims should be evaluated against the underlying record and corroborating evidence.

7. EPSTEIN ISLAND
Epstein owned Little Saint James in the U.S. Virgin Islands. Public records and reporting frequently call it “Epstein Island.” Publicly released records document Epstein's properties, aircraft, associates, and investigations. I have not treated mere association with Epstein, a flight, a photograph, or a named record as evidence of criminal activity.

8. LEGAL AND COURT HISTORY
Trump has been involved in extensive civil and criminal litigation. Major publicly documented matters include New York business-fraud litigation involving the Trump Organization; federal election-related criminal prosecution arising from the 2020 election; federal classified-documents prosecution; New York criminal prosecution concerning falsification of business records; and numerous civil lawsuits and appeals.

Legal status changes over time. A charge or allegation is not equivalent to a conviction, and civil findings are distinct from criminal convictions.
