# Donald J. Trump — Public-Record Dossier

This folder contains a visual PDF edition and a TXT research edition of a public-record dossier covering Donald J. Trump through 17 September 2026.

## Files
- `donald_trump_public_record_dossier_visual_2026-09-17.pdf` — visual PDF edition
- `donald_trump_public_record_dossier_2026-09-17.txt` — text edition

## Scope
The dossier summarizes publicly accessible records, including biography, business activity, presidential service and compensation, public financial disclosures, family business activity since 2024, legal/court records, and publicly released Jeffrey Epstein-related records.

It does **not** contain private messages, hacked communications, private account data, or victim-identifying information. An appearance in an Epstein-related document, photograph, contact book, or flight record is not by itself proof of criminal wrongdoing.

## Research cutoff
17 September 2026.
